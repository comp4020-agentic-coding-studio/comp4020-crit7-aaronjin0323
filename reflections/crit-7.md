# Crit 7 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The biggest breakthrough was noticing that the app treated every clash the
same. When two lectures overlapped, it told me to fix them, even though
neither lecture was offered at any other time. The tests were green, but the
rule was wrong. I spotted it from my own experience as a student: every S2
lecture has only one stream, and 216 course pairs collide that way. After I
raised it, the app lists those clashes as "can't be avoided" and warns before
you add a course that would cause one.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I want to start from a problem I actually have, not a feature list. This app
began with my own frustration at not being able to plan a timetable before
enrolling.

My role also changed. I spent this week directing and judging what an agent
built, not typing it. What I contributed was knowing what "right" meant.

Most of all, I want to trust my knowledge of the problem as a user over a
green test suite. Passing tests only prove the rules that were written down;
they can't say whether those rules match how the ANU timetable really works.
